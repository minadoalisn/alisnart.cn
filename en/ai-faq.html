PROJECT PLANNING

Questions to settle before design begins.

Clear site information produces a useful proposal. These answers define the inputs, constraints, and decisions required before visuals, engineering, and quotation.

PRACTICAL ANSWERS

Start with conditions,
not a reference image.

What should the first project brief contain?

Include the site location, photographs or video, a plan with dimensions, visitor route, available power, budget range, target opening date, and intended operating model.

Can a reference image produce a firm quotation?

No. It can establish visual direction, but size, structure, weather exposure, foundation, power, control, transport, lifting, access, and maintenance must be verified.

How is outdoor durability decided?

Protection is specified as a system: enclosure, connectors, drainage, heat management, cable routing, control cabinets, service access, and local environmental loads.

How should an interactive installation be planned?

Define the trigger, visitor capacity, response time, fallback mode, operating hours, supervision, and maintenance access before selecting sensors or control hardware.

Does Alisn Art work outside Guangdong?

Yes. Projects across China and international enquiries are assessed according to location, approvals, site access, installation responsibility, and local operation requirements.

What happens after the first review?

The next step may be a concept direction, a site survey, a sample, a technical coordination package, or a phased budget. The correct route depends on the evidence available.

PROJECT INPUTS

Send the site information first.

A plan, current photographs, operating requirements, budget range, and target opening date are enough to begin a useful review.
