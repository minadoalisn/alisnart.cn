Custom light installations

What to confirm before fabrication

AlisnART's service scope includes concept design, installation fabrication, lighting control and commissioning. For a custom order, confirm the drawings, fabrication responsibilities, test records and site interfaces in writing before production.

Prepare your project brief
Scope first

What belongs in a fabrication proposal?

Describe the installation, its location and how it will operate. The proposal should identify who supplies the structure, lighting, controls, transport and site work. A service description is not evidence that every process is performed in one factory.

Company identity and service description: Guangdong Alisn Photoelectric Technology Co., Ltd. and AlisnART services.

  • Structure and finish: overall dimensions, connection drawings, material grades, surface finish, transport sections and lifting points.
  • Lighting and controls: luminaire and driver models, power schedule, control protocol, operating modes and interface with existing show equipment.
  • Site responsibilities: foundations, incoming power, drainage, access equipment, installation supervision and commissioning attendance.
  • Serviceability: accessible drivers, replaceable modules, inspection openings, spare-part list and maintenance instructions.
Buyer verification

Ask for evidence tied to your order

Before production

Agree on approved drawings, a material and component schedule, finish samples and any representative lighting mock-up. Identify subcontracted work and the responsible party for each inspection. For outdoor use, ask the project designer to define the structural and electrical design criteria for the actual site.

Water exposure, wind, corrosion, public contact and maintenance access are project inputs—not a universal performance rating for every installation.

Before dispatch and handover

Agree on the inspection plan: dimensions, finish, assembly, electrical checks, lighting scenes, control interfaces, labels and packing. Record the tested configuration, acceptance criteria, results and unresolved items. Factory checks do not replace site commissioning.

Request order-specific fabrication photos, test records and the agreed handover documents. Confirm which project references are completed work and which are concept references.

Warranty coverage, exclusions, response arrangements and delivery milestones must be stated in the project agreement. This page does not establish a standard warranty length, guaranteed response time, fixed lead time or independently verified factory area.

Quotation inputs

Send a brief that can be priced

Start with the site location, marked-up photos or drawings, approximate installation dimensions, intended operating hours and target opening date. Mark unknown inputs as “to be confirmed” rather than estimating them as facts.

Chinese-language project brief checklist · cost scope and exclusions

  1. Installation type, location, dimensions and quantity.
  2. Weather exposure, public access and existing foundations or fixing points.
  3. Available power, control system and required lighting or interaction modes.
  4. Budget range, delivery destination, target dates and installation responsibilities.
  5. Required drawings, samples, inspections, spares and handover records.
