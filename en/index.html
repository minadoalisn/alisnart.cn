01 · READ THE SITE

Where people arrive. Where they stop.

Confirm entrances, primary sightlines, queue space, fire access, power, and maintenance routes before choosing an object.

02 · SHAPE THE STORY

Give the place its own visual language.

Use local culture, architecture, landscape, and operating rhythm instead of applying one generic tunnel or sea of lights everywhere.

03 · ENGINEER THE RESULT

Design maintenance into the first drawing.

Structure, weather protection, control circuits, access panels, transport, lifting, and operating modes are considered before final quotation.

ENGINEERING

buildable and maintainable.

Materials, structure, power, controls, protection, installation, and warranty scope are confirmed from project-specific evidence—not copied from a generic concept page.

Structure and foundation

Dimensions, weight, environmental loads, site conditions, shipping sections, and lifting access.

Power and control

Normal, festival, maintenance, and emergency modes with clearly defined control locations and circuits.

Protection and service
